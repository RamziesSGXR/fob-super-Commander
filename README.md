# FOB Super Commander

*Build your base where managed democracy needs to be managed.*

Build and run your faction's base for Super Earth. A browser game for the **Super Earth Special Forces** community, in the same look as [Orbital Fury](https://orbitalfury.com/) and [Super Orbital Simulator](https://ramziessgxr.github.io/super-orbital-simulator/).

> **Prototype 0.3** (the version shows on the title screen and in the menu). A training and staging camp with supply lines, roads, trucks, a daily routine, day and night, three Ministries and three rival factions, 36 buildings, a crest maker or your own logo, and troopers in your colours. Your base saves on your device.

You command a loyal sub-faction of Super Earth: your name, your crest, your colours, your base. The base turns recruits into troopers for the front. Every week the Federation hands out supplies, and the better your standing with its Ministries, the bigger your share. Three other loyal factions on the planet want the same supplies.

## ▶ Play

The game is at `https://ramziessgxr.github.io/fob-super-Commander/`, served from the `main` branch. Work in progress lives on `dev` until it is pushed live. It runs in a browser on a computer, or on a phone or tablet held sideways. There is nothing to install.

## How to play

Like a city builder, the base runs itself: you lay it out and it gets on with it. Nothing needs a click to keep moving.

- **Found your faction.** Name it, name the base, pick a motto. The crest maker has 11 shapes, 17 symbols (including the Helldiver fist), 9 patterns, up to four letters and four colours, or upload your own logo (up to 2 MB, shrunk to 128 px, kept on your device). Pick your troopers' uniform, helmet and armband, and whether they wear the crest on the shoulder. Change any of it later from **MENU**.
- **Recruits in, troopers out.** Every morning recruits arrive with the supply pelican and fill your empty bunks: more housing, more recruits. From 08:00 to 20:00 they train on the Firing Range (fastest, needs an instructor and ammo), the Obstacle Course, the Parade Ground or in the Citizenship Hall. At 100 points they're troopers. At 16:00 a transport takes trained troopers from the landing pad to meet the Ministry of Defence's deployment order, paying 40 supplies each.
- **Supply lines.** Rations, E-710 fuel and ammo arrive on the supply pelican at 07:00, enough to fill your storage, billed automatically. Forklifts (one at the HQ and one per Supply Depot) and trucks (two per Motor Pool) carry them from the pad to storage, and from storage to the mess halls, generators, firing ranges and patrol offices that use them. Every vote eats a ration a day, at a mess (24 seats), a franchise (16) or the HQ (6). Run short and votes go hungry, generators stop and the range falls silent.
- **Roads.** Pick Dirt Road or Paved Road at the start of the Expansion bar and drag across the ground. Vehicles go 1.6 times as fast on dirt and 2.4 times on paved, and people walk a little faster. Short paved roads between the pad, the depots and the buildings that use them keep everything stocked. Remove Road gives half back.
- **Landing pads and the Comms Tower.** Your first pad is free of paperwork. Every Comms Tower lets you run two more. Each pad gets its own pelican, so more pads mean bigger morning deliveries, more recruits and more seats on the afternoon transports.
- **Science.** A Science Lab (needs a Comms Tower) researches one upgrade at a time while it is staffed and powered: Long-Range Radio, Reinforced Shelving, Drill Sergeant Manuals, Patriotic Photovoltaics, Heavy-Lift Pelicans, a Third Truck, Bureau Asphalt, Fast Recon Vehicles and Armoured Doctrine. Pick projects in the **SCIENCE** tab; more labs research faster.
- **Briefings and armour.** Before any patrol leaves, the squad leader gathers his team by the Patrol Office (or the HQ) and gives them the plan, then they march out the gate together. With Fast Recon Vehicles researched, a Vehicle Bay keeps an FRV, and with Armoured Doctrine a tank. Tick them on a special operation card to take them along: better odds, fewer casualties, some fuel, and a two-day wait if one is wrecked. Armoured assaults only turn up once you have a Vehicle Bay.
- **Standing patrols.** A Patrol Office sends squads of three trained troopers out every morning at 08:00 and brings them home, on its own. Set how many squads it keeps out (0 to 3) when you select it. It keeps those troopers back from the transports. Special operations still turn up in the Defence panel if you want to send one by hand.
- **Three Ministries, three kinds of standing.** Bottom right, with what each has waiting.

  | Ministry | Wants | How it's scored |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | Defence | Trained troopers shipped on time, patrols that come back | Each deployment order completed, each patrol won; a missed order costs standing |
  | Expansion | Supply lines that never run dry, and growth | A weekly audit: buildings stocked, votes fed, a bunk for everyone, and a base that keeps growing |
  | Truth | A base that inspires | A weekly inspection: morale (35%), propaganda (45%) and condition (20%) |

- **The week.** Seven days, two minutes each at normal speed. At the end of it Super Earth ranks the four factions by total standing and splits the allocation (1st gets 1.5×, 4th gets 0.6×), and the higher you rank the more of your empty bunks each morning's recruits fill. Then upkeep is paid.
- **Time.** The clock and date sit bottom left. `[` and `]` slow down and speed up (½× to 8×), `1` `2` `3` pick 1×, 2× and 4×, `Space` pauses.
- **The daily routine.** At reveille (05:00 to 08:00) every vote lines up on the plaza round the Super Earth flag and salutes. Then training and duty, mess at 11:30, more training, free time, and lights out at 22:00, when everyone finds a bunk; votes without one sleep outdoors by the HQ. Generator and motor pool crews work nights. During reveille everything stops: forklifts and trucks park while their drivers salute. At night street lamps and doorways light up and the comms masts blink red.
- **The flag plaza.** The open ground round the Super Earth flag holds two votes per tile. Too small, and the overflow stands in the flowerbeds, morale drops and the inspector writes it down. Select the flag to see the plaza and **MOVE FLAG** (100 supplies).
- **The Bureau** writes every week: memos, casualty form letters, and a ledger (STANDINGS) of the allocation before its handling fee and Presidential consulting. It counts votes, not people: "Two votes have been delivered to your base."
- **Fast food.** One chain per base by exclusive franchise: Big Liberty Burger (huge morale, huge upkeep), Patriot Clucks (cheap, but everyone works 8% slower) or Bureau Cola & Fries (little morale, but a weekly kickback). Each feeds 16.
- **Falling behind never ends the game.** A low rank means less supply, so upkeep goes unpaid and buildings wear down, morale slips and inspections suffer. You can always climb back.
- **Sound.** Wind, a bugle for reveille and lights out, pelicans, building and a ping for every message. **SND** in the top bar turns it off.

## How it's built

Plain JavaScript modules, no framework and no build step. The 3D view is three.js, vendored as one file in `js/vendor/`. Every building, person, vehicle and crest is drawn in code, and there are no model or image files. The look (palette, type, panels and buttons) carries over from Orbital Fury and Super Orbital Simulator.

```
index.html          the page, the title, the faction editor, the HUD
css/style.css       the Orbital Fury theme plus the base-builder HUD
js/config.js        every tuning number: buildings, Ministries, missions, rivals, the week
js/sim.js           the simulation: buildings, crews, power, supply lines and vehicles, training,
                    deployments, patrols, audits, inspections, rivals, the week, saving (no DOM, no 3D)
js/path.js          road-aware pathfinding shared by vehicles and people
js/audio.js         synthesised sound
js/life.js          the ant farm: troopers, forklifts, pelicans, crates, inspectors, ceremonies
js/models.js        every building, person and vehicle, built from simple shapes
js/logo.js          the crest maker
js/view.js          the 3D view: rotating near-isometric camera, ground, the wire, picking
js/demo.js          the finished base behind the title screen
js/main.js          the loop, input, HUD, Ministries panel, faction editor, saves
js/rng.js, noise.js seeded random and value noise (from Super Orbital Simulator)
test/headless.mjs   plays a base without a browser and prints the standings week by week
test/ui.mjs         clicks through the real page on a desktop and on a phone held sideways
```

**Running your own copy:** serve this folder with any local web server (`npm start`, then open <http://localhost:8080>). Opening `index.html` straight from disk won't work, because browsers block ES modules on `file://`. Add `?play` to skip the title, and `&seed=N` to fix the map.

**Tests:** `npm run sim` needs only Node. It plays 14 weeks with a simple bot, and `npm run sim:idle` shows the soft decline of a base that builds nothing. For the browser test, run `npm install` and `npm start`, then `npm run ui` in another terminal.

**Saves:** the base saves on your device every 20 seconds and when you leave. **MENU → COPY SAVE CODE** gives you the whole base as text to keep or move to another device, and **LOAD SAVE CODE** brings it back.
