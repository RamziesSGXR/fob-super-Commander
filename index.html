Field manual

HOW TO PLAY

The job. You command a loyal faction of Super Earth: a training camp that turns recruits into troopers and ships them to the front. You land with a command post, a flag, open ground, and a loan from the Bureau that paid for all of it. Grow the base, pay the Bureau back, and outrank three rival factions. Nothing ends the game: a base you leave alone stops growing but keeps running, and a bad week can always be climbed out of.

Landing. Pick a spot on the planet map. Water and cliffs can't be built on, but they shut the base in like a wall. Violet ground is E-710 for an Extractor; rust is ore for a Mine. You start with an outpost charter (the dashed square); bigger charters come later.

Building. Draw roads first (Expansion bar, drag; G changes snapping). Buildings snap to plots along roads, door to the street. Flags, banners and other small things go anywhere. Draw a wall round it all on the Defence bar; a road through a wall is a gate. Large buildings open a design panel: three designs and add-on parts, chosen before you place them.

Money. Supplies build, pave and pay daily upkeep and the morning deliveries. Credits pay citizen wages, research and Ledger requests. Next to each in the top bar is the net change per day. The LEDGER tab shows every income and cost, your loans and the overdraft. Credits may run into overdraft for a fee; past the limit the staff go unpaid.

The routine runs itself. Recruits arrive with the 07:00 pelican and train on the range, the course and the parade ground. Trained troopers ship out at 16:00 to fill the Ministry of Defence's orders. Trucks and forklifts carry rations, fuel and ammo along your roads. The day follows the Army: First Call 06:00, Reveille 06:30, Retreat 17:00, Taps 21:00.

Standing. Three Ministries rank you each week, and the better your rank, the bigger the allocation:

  • DEFENCE: ship troopers, run patrols and special operations, keep the wall watched and the gates manned.
  • EXPANSION: build, fill the base, keep supplies flowing, repay the Bureau.
  • TRUTH: morale, propaganda and buildings in good repair, judged at the weekly inspection.

The desk. Between deliveries there is always paperwork. Citizens and officials file requests. The Ministries call for goods by a deadline: set them aside or pay for ignoring them. Trooper files need grading. All of it is in the Ledger and none of it is required, but it all pays.

Growing. A Comms Tower lets you run more landing pads, and a Science Lab researches upgrades. A Fort charter and then a Major Base charter (Expansion tab) widen the ground you may build on and unlock the largest buildings.

Controls. Drag to move, scroll or pinch to zoom, Q E or right-drag to rotate. R turns a building, Shift-click places several. Space pauses, [ ] or 1 2 3 set the speed, M opens the Ministries, P is photo mode, H hides the interface. Sound and volume are in the MENU.
