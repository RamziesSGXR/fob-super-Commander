Field manual

HOW TO PLAY

You command a loyal sub-faction of Super Earth. Every week (two and a half minutes) the Federation hands out supplies, and the better your standing, the bigger your share. Three other factions on the planet want the same supplies.

Your standing comes from three Ministries, and they all want something different:

  • DEFENCE wants patrols. Send squads out from the Ministries panel. They march out of the gate and come back, with a medal or a few fewer.
  • EXPANSION wants requisitions filled. Pump E-710, turn it into shells, and ship them from the landing pad before the deadline.
  • TRUTH inspects your base once a week. It wants high morale, lots of propaganda and buildings in good repair.

They pull against each other. Troops on patrol aren't working the pumps. The munitions plant is loud and keeps people awake. A statue costs what a barracks costs. Fall behind and your allocation shrinks, upkeep goes unpaid and the base wears down, but nothing ends: you can always climb back.

Controls. Drag to move around, scroll or pinch to zoom, Q E or right-drag (two-finger twist) to rotate. Pick a building at the bottom, R turns it, click to build. Click a building to select it. Space pauses, M opens the Ministries, H hides the interface for screenshots.
